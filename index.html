02Asuit

Bringing Scrum to a €2M network renewal across 17 healthcare facilities.

Technical Program Manager Intern · Asuit (Provincial Healthcare Provider) · Apr – Aug 2025

I joined a €2M, 2-year program renewing the network infrastructure of the province's healthcare facilities, where aging equipment kept failing under growing data traffic. The plan lived in a fragmented set of Gantt charts that couldn't absorb weekly changes in budget, suppliers and priorities, so I proposed running it in Scrum instead.

  • Secured buy-in from my tutor, a senior network engineer, and the head of department, then restructured the program into 2-week sprints with a shared product backlog.
  • Prioritized the rollout with a 6-person team of network engineers and architects, scoring each site on 5 criteria: user impact, downtime, effort, expected benefit and budget.
  • Ran sprint planning, daily stand-ups and retrospectives on a shared Kanban board, giving every engineer full visibility over the backlog.
  • Coordinated 4 vendors across interdependent workstreams (network hardware, infrastructure upgrades and the Telnet-to-SSH security migration), tracking dependencies and delivery.
  • The change stuck: at the end of my internship, the head of department funded formal project-management training for the whole team so they could keep working in Agile.

01SkillForge

An e-sports coaching marketplace: the right idea, validated with data, halted with discipline.

Solo · Product Discovery · Dec 2023 – Feb 2024 ● Halted before build, on purpose

A platform connecting e-sports players with pro-grade coaching content at a fraction of 1:1 cost. The market was broken two ways: skilled players aren't necessarily good teachers, and live coaching at €150–250/hr priced out most players.

  • Surveyed 350 players across League of Legends, Valorant, Fortnite and Rainbow Six and ran 25 in-depth interviews. 64% confirmed the accessibility barrier, and cross-title demand expanded the addressable market 4×.
  • Mapped the competitive gap (Metafy, Coachify, Tapin.gg, Udemy) and designed a content-first model: pros record structured courses sold on commission, projecting +20% margins by targeting retired rather than active pros.
  • Stress-tested the model and found the bottleneck: creator acquisition and a classic cold-start problem: no content, no users, no creators.
  • Halted before any build. A deliberate build-vs-no-build call: resource discipline over sunk-cost momentum.
