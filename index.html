03100flows

Designing the “Augmented PM” — a systematic study of how AI is reshaping product management.

Research internship · University of Trento · Dec 2025 – Present ● In progress — research, not a shipped product

Overview

A 250-hour research internship and thesis into how AI is reshaping every domain of product management — planning, risk, resources, stakeholder communication and decision-making. I paired 60+ academic papers with primary research: 8 in-depth interviews with PMs who use AI every day, across enterprise and research organizations.

Research & insight

  • Interviewed 8 PMs at Amazon, Microsoft, FBK, Salesforce, SumUp, Yahoo! and Workday — mapping where AI already creates value (drafting & stakeholder comms, sentiment and data analysis, automation and “vibe coding”) and where it still breaks down.
  • Isolated the sharpest gap: predictive risk analysis is seen as AI's biggest opportunity — moving PM from reactive to proactive — yet is still the least used in practice.
  • Captured the caveats first-hand: only ~10% of companies see measurable benefit today, data security and maturity are real brakes, and “human in the loop” is non-negotiable.

Solution & impact

  • Distilled it into a product thesis — “Augmented PM”: AI absorbs the administrative and analytical burden so the PM operates strategically, always keeping the human in the loop. I designed the full 100flows interface in Figma from that framework.
  • The literature quantifies the opportunity — roughly −50% decision-cycle time, −72% communication overhead and 3.6× stakeholder engagement.
  • Next: a multi-agent AI risk engine framed around reasoning under uncertainty, which I'm pressure-testing with senior PMs at Microsoft and Amazon.

01SkillForge

An e-sports coaching marketplace — the right idea, validated with data, halted with discipline.

Solo · Product Discovery · Dec 2023 – Feb 2024 ● Halted before build — on purpose

Overview

A platform connecting e-sports players with pro-grade coaching content at a fraction of 1:1 cost. The market was broken two ways: skilled players aren't necessarily good teachers, and live coaching at €150–250/hr priced out most players.

Research

  • Surveyed 350 players across League of Legends, Valorant, Fortnite and Rainbow Six and ran 25 in-depth interviews — 64% confirmed the accessibility barrier, and cross-title demand expanded the addressable market 4×.
  • Mapped the competitive gap (Metafy, Coachify, Tapin.gg, Udemy) and designed a content-first model: pros record structured courses sold per-course on commission — projecting +20% margins by targeting retired vs. active pros.

Decision

  • Stress-tested the model and found the bottleneck: creator acquisition and a classic cold-start problem — no content → no users → no creators.
  • I halted before any build — a deliberate build-vs-no-build call, resource discipline over sunk-cost momentum.
  • The structured thinking, not a shipped product, is what SkillForge demonstrates.
