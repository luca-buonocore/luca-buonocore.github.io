01A/B

A PM simulator game: one quarter in the job, before you land it.

Product Manager · Oct 2026 – Present ● Live

Play the demo ↗

Wireframes · Lo-Fi

Aspiring PMs rarely get to practice the job before they land it: courses teach frameworks, real life teaches trade-offs. So I built A/B, a browser game where you run one quarter as a PM. You interview users, write the PRD, plan sprints, launch, and live with the metrics. I shipped the first version in 3 days with one engineer, built it in public with real PMs as playtesters, and used an A/B test to fix the point where players were giving up.

02Asuit

Bringing Scrum to a €2M network renewal across 17 healthcare facilities.

Technical Program Manager Intern · Provincial Healthcare Provider · Apr – Aug 2025

The province's healthcare network was failing under growing traffic, and its renewal was planned in Gantt charts that broke every week. I convinced my tutor and the head of department to try Scrum instead, then ran the rollout with the engineers in two-week sprints, deciding together which facilities to fix first and keeping every vendor aligned. The change outlived my internship: the department paid for project-management training so the team could keep working that way.

03Groove

A nightlife-discovery MVP, from concept to a €100K conditional offer.

Co-Founder & Product Manager · Feb 2024 – Feb 2025

Wireframes · Lo-Fi

Young people kept wasting their Saturday nights in clubs that weren't what they expected, simply because the information wasn't there. I co-founded Groove to fix that, wrote the PRD and led a cross-functional team from concept to MVP. Before building any AI, we faked the recommendations by hand with a Wizard-of-Oz test, and what it showed was enough to convince angel investors.

01SkillForge

An e-sports coaching marketplace: validated with data, halted with discipline.

Solo · Product Discovery · Dec 2023 – Feb 2024 ● Halted on purpose

E-sports coaching was priced for the few: most of the players I talked to simply couldn't afford it. I designed a marketplace where retired pros sell recorded courses instead, then stress-tested it and found a loop I couldn't break: without content there are no users, and without users no creator joins. So I stopped before building anything. Knowing when not to build is part of the job.
